H/77 WEBSITE — DROP 01
Open index.html in your browser to preview the site.

Files:
- index.html: homepage
- shop.html: products
- product.html: product details
- cart.html: demo cart using browser localStorage
- about.html / contact.html
- css/style.css
- js/products.js / js/app.js

The prices are placeholders and the checkout is a demo. Replace them before launch.
To add real product photos, put your images in the images folder and update the product cards/backgrounds in style.css or app.js.
